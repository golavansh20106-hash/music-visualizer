STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

How to Capture PC Audio

01

Pure PC Audio Loopback

The visualizer captures whatever music or sound is playing on your PC (Spotify, YouTube, games, media players). Microphone inputs are 100% blocked and excluded.

02

Share System Audio

Click "Connect PC Audio". In the browser screen-sharing dialog, select "Entire Screen" and make sure the "Also share system audio" checkbox is checked.

03

Zero Device Menus, Minimalist HUD

Press F for fullscreen. Controls automatically hide after 3 seconds so you can enjoy pure audio visual art without distractions.

ONE-CLICK SETUP REQUIRED

Check "Also share system audio"

When the browser asks what screen to share, Chrome/Edge has an un-checked box at the bottom-left:

Entire Screen Window Chrome Tab
Also share system audio ← CHECK THIS BOX

Note: If you select the "Window" tab, Windows disables audio sharing. Always choose "Entire Screen" or a specific "Chrome Tab".
